About / Founder Story

Consistency beats cleverness. Speed beats perfection.

I spent 39 years in hospitality before I ever touched a content calendar — on the floor, in the kitchen, and in the back office running P&Ls for fourteen different restaurants. I know what it looks like when a dining room is actually working, and I know what it costs an owner when the small things — a slow reply to a bad review, a dead Instagram, a menu photo nobody's updated in two years — get pushed to the bottom of the list because service, staffing, and suppliers come first. They always will. That's the job.

Martinez Consulting Co exists to take that one thing off your plate and run it the way I ran everything else: on a schedule, with a plan, and with someone who actually answers the phone. Not agency jargon. Not a template with your logo pasted on it. Restaurant-floor knowledge applied to your online presence.
